Brushes bundled with SAM - Stickman Animation Maker
===================================================

These brush collections are third-party works. They are NOT part of SAM and are NOT
covered by SAM's own license (PolyForm Strict): each one remains under the license
stated below, and you may use, copy, modify and share it under those terms, inside
or outside SAM.

The .mbrush files were produced by SAM's own "Import brush" converter from the Krita
.bundle files named below. The conversion keeps each preset's brush tip, size,
spacing, tip angle, opacity and random rotation/size/opacity; Krita's pressure
curves, textures, blending modes and the smudge, eraser, particle, deform and
experiment engines have no equivalent in SAM and were left out. Original files are
available from the sources listed.

License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal, as stated in the article's License

Nylnook v1.1.mbrush
-------------------
"Nylnook Brushes Presets v1.1" by Camille Bissuel (Nylnook)
https://github.com/nylnook/nylnook-krita-brushes

Brush tips shared with Krita's default resources
------------------------------------------------
Several tips in the David Revoy and Nylnook v1.1 collections are identical to tips
distributed with Krita's default resources (for example chalk_chisel_random_small,
impressionism_brush, random-debris, scratches_rough, splats_large, A_concrete,
A_eroded-cercle-anim, A_mineral-cristal, Z_Triangle_shape_1). Krita's resource
bundles label them CC0, while Krita's data README states that the .gbr/.gih/.vbr
brushes shipped with Krita come from David Revoy's "Chaos and Evolutions" brush set
and Ramon Miranda's "GIMP Paint Studio" and are licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution 3.0 Unported (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), attributed
to David Revoy (www.davidrevoy.com) and the Blender Foundation (www.blender.org).
To satisfy either reading, those tips are credited here accordingly: they are by
David Revoy and Ramon Miranda, used under CC BY 3.0, and were converted (format
change, and downscaled to at most 512 px) from Krita's file formats into SAM's.
No other terms are imposed on them.
