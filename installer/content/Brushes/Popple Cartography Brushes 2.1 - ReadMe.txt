ABOUT THIS SET
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

The signs symbols within this brush set have been extracted from (and yes, this is its real name) “A Map of the British Empire in America with the French and Spanish Settlements adjacent thereto” by Henry Popple—an 18th Century map that details the British Colonies in America in 1746. There’s a fresh approach to maps here that will work perfect for any fantasy map—plus it has swamps! As we learned from Tolkien, every fantasy map is incomplete unless they feature a few swamps.

Thanks for download this brush set and I hope you find them useful.

More details on this set and other brushes can be found at: kmalexander.com
